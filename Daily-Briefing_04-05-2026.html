📚 30-Minute Learning Focus — Drone & Regs
Today: CAA Drone Regulations, A2 CofC Prep & Airspace Awareness

Your CAA Flyer ID and Operator ID are done — that’s the foundation. The A2 Certificate of Competency (A2 CofC) is the next commercial unlock. It allows you to fly in the Open A2 sub-category: closer to people, in more conditions, with more commercial opportunities than the basic registration alone. PDT Ltd (Liam McGrath, 07768 495884) is your training contact. Today’s 30 minutes builds the knowledge base you need before booking.

  • Action A (Core — 15 min): Read CAA’s Drone Code Summary. Go to caa.co.uk/drones. Read the “Know Before You Fly” section and the sub-category breakdown (A1, A2, A3). Understand what A2 unlocks: 30m minimum distance from uninvolved people (reduced from 50m A3), operations in populated areas. Note the DJI Air 3S classification under Open sub-category rules. This is the most important 15 minutes of today.
  • Action B (Practical — 10 min): Review the NOTAM System. Go to nats.aero/ais/notams or the NATS drone app. Search for NOTAMs around Invergordon (ICAO region EGPE Inverness area). Understand what a NOTAM looks like and how to read the effective dates, altitude limits, and coordinates. This is a mandatory pre-flight skill for any commercial operation.
  • Action C (Planning — 5 min): Map Today’s Port Zone. Open DJI Fly or Drone Assist (FPVUK app). Find Invergordon port on the map. Identify the restriction zones around port infrastructure. Note where the “green” (unrestricted) zones begin. This is your future flight planning exercise for ship photography once your A2 CofC is confirmed.

Free Resources: caa.co.uk/drones (official CAA drone hub) · nats.aero/ais/notams (NOTAMs) · fpvuk.org (FPVUK drone insurance & mapping app, member ID in hand)
