📚 30-Minute Learning Focus — Photography
Today: Maritime & Landscape Photography — Composition, Light & Commercial Use

Photography is one of Steve’s strongest immediate commercial opportunities — the Cromarty Firth cruise season provides a ready-made subject that very few photographers are positioned to capture consistently. Today’s 30 minutes prepares you for tomorrow’s Majestic Princess shoot and builds the commercial mindset to turn that shoot into income.

  • Action A (10 min): Study the Rule of Thirds for maritime photography. Go to Digital Photography School (digital-photography-school.com) and search “rule of thirds landscape.” Apply it specifically to a ship scenario: position the ship at one of the four intersection points, let the horizon sit at the upper or lower third (not the middle), and use the firth as a leading line into the frame. This single technique will transform your ship shots from snapshots into sellable images.
  • Action B (10 min): Learn the “Exposure Triangle” for moving subjects. Go to Cambridge in Colour (cambridgeincolour.com/tutorials). Read the shutter speed tutorial. For Majestic Princess tomorrow: shutter speed of at least 1/500s will freeze any gentle ship movement; 1/125s gives a slight motion blur on water that can look dramatic. Understand why faster shutter = darker image, and how ISO compensates at dawn.
  • Action C (10 min): Research stock photography pricing and keywords. Go to Alamy.com, search “Invergordon cruise ship.” Note what existing images sell for and how they are keyworded. Then go to Shutterstock contributor guidelines. Understand the earnings per download model. One well-keyworded shot of Majestic Princess tomorrow could generate passive income for years from cruise tourism buyers, travel magazines, and Princess Cruises themselves.
