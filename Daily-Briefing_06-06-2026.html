💼 Upwork — Winning Proposals That Convert
The 3-Sentence Proposal Formula
1️⃣ Mirror their problem — restate what they need in your own words so they know you read the post. 2️⃣ Your relevant proof — one specific past win that maps directly to their need (e.g. "I ran a Telegram community of 3,000+ for a crypto project and handled daily moderation + AMAs"). 3️⃣ Clear next step — invite a short call or ask one question that shows you're already thinking about their project.

Keep it under 150 words. Long proposals get skipped. Short, specific ones get clicked.
