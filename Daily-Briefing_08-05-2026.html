Today: Freelancing — Niching Down, Pricing With Confidence, and Building a Pipeline That Earns

Freelancing is not about being the best at everything — it is about being the obvious choice for something specific. The freelancers who earn consistently pick a niche narrow enough to be memorable and broad enough to find clients. For you, that niche is already clear: AI content + crypto writing + community management. Today’s 30 minutes is about structuring that into a system you can run every day.

  • Action A (10 min): Define your niche statement. Write one sentence that describes exactly what you do, who you do it for, and what outcome they get. Template: “I help [specific client type] achieve [specific outcome] through [your skill].” Example: “I help Web3 startups and Bitcoin companies produce clear, engaging content that educates their audience and builds community trust — without the technical jargon that loses non-crypto readers.” Write yours now. This statement becomes your Upwork headline, your Bark.com pitch, your LinkedIn bio, and your first line in every proposal. Consistency across all platforms compounds into authority.
  • Action B (10 min): Set your rates with data. Go to upwork.com/freelance-jobs/ai-content-creation and sort by budget. Note what clients are paying for AI content writing (typically £25–£55/hr for solid work, more for specialist niches). Now check cryptojobs.com/content-writing — what are crypto-native companies paying? (£30–£60/hr is realistic for crypto-specific content). Your £28/hr rate is your floor, not your ceiling. On your next Upwork proposal, consider quoting £32–35/hr for crypto or AI writing. Clients who are price-sensitive on basic content are rarely your best clients anyway.
  • Action C (10 min): Build a 5-day proposal habit. The freelancers who earn consistently send proposals every working day, even when they are busy. Create a simple tracker: a notes file with columns for Date, Job Title, Platform, Status. Aim for 1–2 proposals per day. At 5 proposals per week with a 10% interview rate, you get 2 interviews per month. With a 50% close rate, that is 1 new client per month. After 6 months you have a roster. Write your first tracker entry today and submit at least one proposal before you close Upwork.
