🚁 Drone & Regs — UK Airspace Rules for Commercial Drone Operators

Today’s 30-minute session: Master the UK drone regulatory framework and identify exactly what qualifications unlock the commercial work you want.

Why this matters: Every commercial photography commission, every tourism shot of a cruise ship, every paid coastal survey — all require you to operate legally. Understanding the rules is not just compliance. It is the product you sell to clients. Drone operators who can say “I understand BVLOS, PDRA01, GVC, and A2 CofC” command professional rates. Those who cannot are limited to amateur work.

Your 30-minute exercise:

  1. Minutes 0–8: UK Drone Regulation Structure. Open the CAA website (caa.co.uk/drones) and navigate to the Flying for commercial purposes section. Read the three operational categories: Open, Specific, and Certified. Understand which category your current DJI Air 3S operations fall under (Open Category, sub-categories A1 and A3 with your Flyer ID).
  2. Minutes 8–18: A2 CofC vs GVC — what each unlocks. A2 CofC (Certificate of Competency) is an online exam that allows you to fly a Class C2 drone within 5m of uninvolved people in low-speed mode. GVC (General Visual Line of Sight Certificate) is the full ground school + exam qualification that grants Specific Category operational authorisation, including PDRA01 and PDRA02 — essential for commercial coastal and event work near Invergordon. Ask Claude to explain the difference in plain language and write it in your own words.
  3. Minutes 18–26: Map your 3 target flight scenarios. Write down three specific jobs you want to do commercially: (1) Cruise ship photography from Invergordon pier, (2) Highland tourism aerial shots for Explore Invergordon, (3) Coastal inspection or mapping work. For each, identify which regulation applies and which qualification you need to operate legally at commercial proximity.
  4. Minutes 26–30: Call to action. Draft a WhatsApp message to Liam McGrath at PDT Ltd (07768 495884) asking about available A2 CofC and GVC course dates. You do not need to send it today — just draft it. It takes 2 minutes. Having it ready removes the friction to actually booking.
