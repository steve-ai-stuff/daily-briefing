Unlock A2 CofC — Your Commercial Flight Clearance

The A2 Certificate of Competency from the CAA allows you to fly Class C2 drones within 50m of uninvolved people in Open Category operations — a game-changer for commercial aerial photography work near towns, events, and ports like Cromarty Firth. Here's your action plan:

  • Register as operator & get your Flyer ID at register.caa.co.uk (free, 20 questions)
  • Study the A2 CofC theory syllabus — includes air law, meteorology, and drone performance

📚 30-Min Learning Focus — Drone & Regs

CAA Open Category: A1, A2, A3 Sub-Categories & A2 CofC Pathway

Today's session is on the UK CAA Open Category drone regulations — specifically the three sub-categories (A1/A2/A3) and how the A2 Certificate of Competency unlocks commercial operations near people.

📌 Key Concepts to Learn Today:

  • A1: Flying over uninvolved people (with class C0/C1 drones) — no CofC needed but rules still apply
  • A2: Flying close to uninvolved people (C2 drones) — requires the A2 CofC exam
  • A3: Flying away from people and built-up areas — most restrictive operationally
  • Transition rules for legacy drones (no class marking) in 2026
  • The 1:1 rule: in A2, you can fly as close as 50m to uninvolved people in normal mode

🔗 Free Resource: CAA Open Category Rules — official, free, authoritative

⏱️ 30-Minute Exercise:

Go to the CAA website and read the Open Category overview page. Then take a free mock A2 CofC theory test (search "A2 CofC mock test UK" — several free options exist). Aim for 16/20 or above. Note any questions you got wrong and look up the answer in the official CAA guidance. That's your study list for tomorrow.
