🎯 CAA Drone Regulation Focus Today

The UK CAA divides drone operations into Open, Specific, and Certified categories. For most commercial photography work near Invergordon, you'll operate in the Open Category (sub-250g drones) or need an A2 Certificate of Competency (CofC) for operations closer to uninvolved people.
