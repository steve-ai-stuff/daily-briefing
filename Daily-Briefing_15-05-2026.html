📖 Today’s Study Focus: Building a Stock Photography Income Stream

Stock photography is one of the few income streams where your location is a genuine competitive advantage. The Highlands is a premium visual market: landscapes, wildlife, coastal drama, weather, and cultural heritage all command top rates from editorial and commercial buyers worldwide.

Best platforms for Highland photographers in 2026:
• Adobe Stock — highest per-download rates, massive Creative Cloud buyer base. stock.adobe.com/contributor
• Alamy — UK-based, excellent editorial rates. Highland heritage sells particularly well. alamy.com/contributor
• Shutterstock — largest buyer base, volume compensates for lower per-image rate. submit.shutterstock.com
• Getty via iStock — premium tier, rigorous curation, higher earnings per download

What sells from the Highlands: Cromarty Firth panoramas, North Sea light, red deer, highland cattle, castles, mist and fog, dramatic skies, coastal villages, whisky distilleries, fishing boats, cruise ships entering the firth (editorial use — no model release needed).

Action today: Create contributor accounts on Adobe Stock and Alamy. Upload 5–10 of your best existing Highland shots with keyword-rich metadata. The first sale proves the model — passive income builds from there.
