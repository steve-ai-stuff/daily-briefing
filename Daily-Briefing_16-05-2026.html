🎯 30-Min Exercise — Analyse Your Job Search:
  1. Open Google Sheets. Create a new sheet called “Job Search Tracker”. Add columns: Date, Company, Role, Platform, Tier (1/2), Salary, Applied (Y/N), Response (Y/N/Pending) (5 min)
  2. Fill in any jobs you have applied to this week — or if none yet, fill in the top 5 from today’s list as “Pending Application” (5 min)
  3. Add a simple COUNTIF formula: =COUNTIF(F2:F100,"Y") to count applications sent. Add =AVERAGE(E2:E10) to average salary (10 min)
  4. Create one chart: select the Platform column and use Insert → Chart to visualise which job boards have the most relevant roles for you (5 min)
  5. Read: What is “data-driven decision making”? Spend 5 minutes on the Google Analytics Certificate intro page and write down one thing you learned (5 min)
