🌐 Freelancing — The 3-Line Proposal Opener
Win proposals by mirroring the client's problem back in line one
Most freelancers open with "I am an experienced writer…" — clients skim past it. Instead: Line 1 restates their problem in your words ("You need 10 product descriptions that sound human, not AI-spun"). Line 2 proves you can fix it (one relevant sample or fact). Line 3 proposes a tiny first step ("I can send the first two by Wednesday — want a sample paragraph now?"). On Upwork this doubles reply rates because it shows you actually read the brief. Use it on every proposal this week.
