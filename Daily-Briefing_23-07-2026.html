Drone & Regs: Today’s Tip

Flying Near Invergordon Port — What’s Legal

⚠️
Port FRZ (Flight Restriction Zone)

The port and immediate berths fall under FRZ rules. You need Specific Category Operational Authorisation and ATC approval to fly inside. Do not launch from the pier or dock area.

✅
Shoreline Positions ≥150m from Port

Open-Category A2 or A3 may apply at sufficient distance. Check NATS Drone Assist and the CAA DroneCode app for the exact boundary before every flight. The B9175 layby opposite the pier offers a clean sightline with clearance.

📸
Commercial Opportunity Today (No Drone Required)

2,894 TUI passengers are ashore until 19:00. Ground-level photography of Mein Schiff 2 against Invergordon’s iconic red cranes is commercial-ready content — no airspace authorisation needed. Sell prints or licence for tourism content.

📋
Key Regulation: CAA Operational Authorisation (OA)

For Specific Category work near FRZ/congested areas, submit a SORA via the CAA portal. Allow 28+ days. An OA unlocks paid commercial drone work in restricted sites — worth pursuing once your A2 CofC is in hand.
