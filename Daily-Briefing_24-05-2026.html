📋 2026 UK Drone Law: What Changed on 1 January — Your Quick Ref
The new UK class marking system is now live. Here is what it means for you as a pilot in the Cromarty Firth area:
  • UK Class Marking (UK0–UK6) — All new drones placed on the UK market from 1 Jan 2026 carry a class mark. Your operational privileges depend on this mark, not weight alone.
  • A2 CofC expanded privileges — With A2 CofC you can now fly as close as 30m to people (down from 50m) in the A2 Near People subcategory. That means closer drone footage of cruise ship arrivals at the Invergordon berths.
  • Remote ID mandatory — All new UK1–UK6 class-marked drones must broadcast Remote ID. Legacy drones remain legal but are operationally restricted without it.
  • Night flying — From 1 Jan 2026, any drone flown at night must display a green flashing light. This opens up dramatic golden-hour and evening shots at Cromarty Firth — provided your kit is compliant.
  • Flyer ID — Required for any drone 100g or over. Free, 20 questions, 30 minutes — and the starting gate to all other commercial work.
Invergordon open zone: most of the Cromarty Firth is Open Category / A2 compliant. Watch NOTAM around the Port and any active military or industrial activity at Nigg Bay.
