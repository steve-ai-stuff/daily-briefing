🛒 Freelancing Platforms — Two platforms, one offer
Stop spreading thin. Pick two platforms and run the same productised offer on both.
The four big ones work differently and that difference should decide where you sit, not which name you recognise.

Upwork — clients post, you bid using Connects. Highest ceiling for ongoing retainers, worst time-per-win ratio. Your crypto and customer-success history reads as real experience here.
Fiverr — you post, clients buy. No bidding, no proposals, but you must package the work as a fixed thing with a fixed price. Best match for drone stills, photo editing, and a defined AI-automation setup.
Contra — commission-free for freelancers, portfolio-led, skews toward design, content and marketing. Good shop window with a lower volume of buyers.
PeoplePerHour — UK-weighted, smaller, and UK clients paying in sterling means no currency spread and easier calls in your timezone.

The mistake is six half-built profiles. Fiverr plus PeoplePerHour is the strongest pair for you: one sells packages globally, the other reaches UK buyers who can speak to you at 10am. Write the offer once, list it twice, and judge it on impressions after two weeks — not on how it feels.

📚 30-Min Learning Focus — Freelancing Platforms
Today: write one productised service and publish it
A productised service is one named deliverable, one price, one turnaround — no scoping call, no hourly debate. It is the single highest-leverage asset on any platform because it sells without you being present. Today you write one and put it live. Suggested offer, built from what you already have: "AI inbox and reply system for small service businesses — set up in 3 days, £250 fixed." You know customer service, you know the tools, and small firms in the Highlands are drowning in email.
📖 Free Resource: Upwork Resource Center
Upwork's own free guides on profile writing, proposals, pricing and getting the first contract — written by the platform that controls the ranking, which makes it the closest thing to the rulebook. upwork.com/resources
🎬 Free Resource: Freelancers Union Resource Hub
Independent, no-sales-pitch material on contracts, rate setting, chasing late payment and the admin side of going solo — the parts platforms never teach you. freelancersunion.org/resources
⏱ Your 30-min structure:
0–5 min — Write the one-line offer: deliverable, price, turnaround. Nothing else.
5–15 min — Write the listing body: who it is for, the three things they get, what you need from them, what it does not include. Plain language, no jargon.
15–22 min — Publish it on Fiverr as a Gig. Imperfect is fine; you can edit it forever.
22–28 min — Copy the same text onto PeoplePerHour as an Offer. Same price in sterling.
28–30 min — Write the two URLs into your tracker. In two weeks you judge impressions and clicks, not feelings.
